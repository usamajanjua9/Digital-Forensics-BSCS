## Course Overview

This course introduces students to the principles, methodologies, and technologies used in digital forensic investigations. It focuses on the systematic identification, acquisition, preservation, examination, analysis, and reporting of digital evidence.

Students will develop both theoretical understanding and practical investigative skills through case studies, demonstrations, Python-based exercises, and forensic analysis tools.

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion, students should be able to:

1. Understand digital forensics principles and investigation procedures.
2. Identify, collect, and preserve digital evidence.
3. Explain forensic imaging, hashing, and chain of custody.
4. Investigate file systems, metadata, logs, and digital artifacts.
5. Apply introductory memory and network forensic techniques.
6. Use forensic tools and Python for evidence examination.
7. Prepare structured forensic investigation reports.
8. Understand legal, ethical, and professional responsibilities.

## Repository Contents

- **Weekly Lectures:** Concepts, theoretical explanations, illustrations, and case studies.
- **Practical Notebooks:** Python-based activities and demonstrations.
- **Class Activities:** Interactive investigations and scenario-based exercises.
- **Quizzes:** Conceptual and analytical assessments.
- **Assignments:** Independent forensic investigation tasks.
- **Group Project:** Practical investigation with documentation and presentation guidelines.
- **Resources:** Recommended readings, tools, and reference material.

## Tools and Technologies

Python | Jupyter Notebook | Autopsy | Wireshark | Volatility 3 | ExifTool | Digital Hashing Utilities

## Learning Approach

The course emphasizes conceptual understanding, investigative reasoning, evidence-based analysis, and guided practical applications.

## Academic Integrity and Ethics

All forensic exercises must be performed on authorized systems, supplied datasets, or simulated environments. Students must respect privacy, data protection principles, and applicable laws.
